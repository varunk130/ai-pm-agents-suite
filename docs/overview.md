# Overview

> 🚧 Skeleton — full content lands in PR 4.

The AI PM Agents Suite is a single home for two complementary collections of AI agents purpose-built for product managers.

## What's inside

1. **A 6-agent orchestrated pipeline** that turns 50 raw customer feedback tickets + 6 months of product metrics into:
   - A prioritized strategic recommendation
   - A complete PRD
   - 5 audience-tailored stakeholder communications

2. **3 standalone single-purpose agents** for everyday PM work:
   - **Decision Engine** — multi-framework strategic analysis
   - **Financial Analyst** — full feature business case with sensitivity analysis
   - **Stakeholder Translator** — 5 audience-tailored versions of any product update

## When to use which

| Situation | Reach for |
|-----------|-----------|
| End-to-end synthesis of qualitative + quantitative data into strategy | The 6-agent pipeline |
| A single decision needs multi-framework analysis | Decision Engine |
| You need a business case for a feature | Financial Analyst |
| You need to communicate a single update to 5 audiences | Stakeholder Translator |
